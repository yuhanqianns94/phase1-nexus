# Phase I Toxicity V3

A research toolkit for organizing Phase I oncology evidence, exploring dose-related toxicity, and evaluating trial designs for single agents and drug combinations.

## What it can do

- **Find and organize evidence** from clinical trial registries and published articles, including available full text, tables, and figures.
- **Extract structured study information**, including treatments, dosing schedules, patient populations, dose cohorts, and dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) outcomes.
- **Review extracted evidence** alongside its source and record human corrections and article relevance decisions.
- **Build a target trial plan** for single-agent or combination therapy, including population, administration, eligibility, and DLT criteria, even before doses are finalized.
- **Explore toxicity risk** across candidate doses and regimens, with uncertainty estimates, historical evidence comparisons, and interactive curves and two-drug risk maps.
- **Simulate BOIN dose-finding trials** and compare dose-selection rates, patient allocation, and toxicity outcomes.
- **Save and export analyses** for further review and research.

The project includes an interactive web interface and command-line tools.

## 中文介绍

本项目面向 I 期肿瘤临床试验研究，支持单药与多药联合方案的文献证据整理、毒性风险探索和试验设计评估。

- 检索和整理临床试验注册信息及发表文献，收集可获取的全文、表格和图片。
- 提取药物、给药方案、患者人群、剂量队列和剂量限制性毒性（DLT）等结构化信息。
- 对照原始证据进行人工复核，记录提取修正及文献相关性判断。
- 创建目标试验方案，支持尚未确定具体剂量的早期设计。
- 比较历史证据，查看不同剂量和联合方案的毒性风险、不确定性、交互式曲线和双药风险图。
- 开展 BOIN 剂量探索试验模拟，评估剂量选择、患者分配和毒性结局。
- 保存和导出分析结果，供进一步复核与研究使用。

提供交互式网页界面和命令行工具。
